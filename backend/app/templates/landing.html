06 / FAQ

What you'll want to know before we talk.

If your business works in its own way, we'll go through it in the personal demo.

Do I keep my current number?

Yes. You keep using your public number. Forwarding and the options available are checked against your phone provider or system.

Can my own receptionist answer first?

Yes. We recommend starting that way: your team answers first and the assistant covers the calls you miss.

Does it answer after hours?

It can, if your phone system supports forwarding after hours.

Does it speak Greek and English?

Every call starts in Greek. Callers can switch to English by asking for it; we check English in your own demo before turning it on for customers.

Which calendar does it work with?

Google Calendar is supported. If you use another booking platform, we'll look together at how it can fit in.

What if it can't help?

It transfers the call or arranges a callback, as agreed, and can take a message for your team.

Do my customers need an app?

No. They call your usual number.

How does it handle calls and personal data?

During setup we agree what it collects, who gets notified and how long data is kept. Sensitive fields and recordings are encrypted. For medical practices it handles admin requests only and never gives medical advice.
