BEFORE YOU SAY HELLO

A few good questions.

Does this demo place a real call?+

No. This website currently shows an interactive simulation. The phone number is validated in your browser and is not stored or sent. A live calling service still needs to be connected.

What information would my assistant need?+

Start with the facts your team uses every day: business hours, services, common questions, and instructions for handling requests. Specific integrations and routing need to be configured before launch.

Can a person take over?+

Human handoff should be part of your call flow. This preview illustrates how requests could be captured; live transfers and callback delivery require a connected phone service.

Can it book appointments in my calendar?+

The examples show appointment requests. Confirmed bookings require a calendar integration and availability checks, which are not connected in this preview.

Can I keep my business phone number?+

That depends on your phone provider and the routing setup. Call forwarding or number connection would be confirmed before activating a live service.
